# Germ Arena — shared art direction for the lobby (home screen)

Game: "Germ Arena", a mobile LANDSCAPE online io-style arena. Players are single-celled
microbes swimming in a giant water-filled petri dish, shooting glossy balls at each other.
We are building the home/lobby screen in the spirit of Brawl Stars: a big, polished hero
character standing on a stage in the centre, over a rich themed background, with UI
panels around the edges.

## Look
- Stylized, chunky, "premium mobile game key art" (Supercell / Brawl Stars polish), but
  set in a MICROSCOPIC underwater world. Soft 3D-ish cartoon rendering, clean shapes.
- Thick dark outline (#04090B) on characters and props.
- Cel-shading with ONE global light: from the TOP-LEFT. Highlights top-left, shadows and
  a thin bright rim light on the bottom-right edge.
- Mood: bioluminescent, dark teal water, glowing accents. Readable, not murky.

## Palette (stay close to these)
- Deep background   #071012, far vignette #020708
- Dish floor        #12262C, dish glass edge #2B5963, grid/rings #1A3942
- Cyan glow accent  #2FE8E0
- Hero body (laym)  #7CFF5B, body light tint ~#C9FFB8, body shadow ~#3D8A2C
- Food / glossy balls #D9FFC0 with #F6FFEE core
- Warm accent (use sparingly) #FFB43D
- Outline           #04090B

## HARD RULES — an image that breaks one of these is rejected
1. Microbes have NO FACE. No eyes, no pupils, no mouth, no eyebrows, no "cute face".
   Appeal comes only from silhouette, glossy jelly material and pose.
2. No text, letters, numbers, logos, UI, watermarks or signatures anywhere.
3. No humans, no hands, no lab equipment in the foreground.
